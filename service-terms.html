What a Safe Haven consultation includes, and what it does not.

Scope of consultation

Assessment, advice
and recommendations.

Safe Haven provides advisory consultations, including nursery safety observations, furniture placement recommendations and functional room layout guidance.

Our services do not include furniture assembly, installation, physical relocation, wall anchoring or modification of furniture and fixtures. Clients are responsible for arranging and ensuring the safe completion of any recommended changes by appropriately qualified professionals where required.

A consultation is personalised guidance for your home. It is not a certification, guarantee of safety or substitute for current product instructions and relevant professional advice.
